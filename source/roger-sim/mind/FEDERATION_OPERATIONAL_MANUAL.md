## 4. How to get icons on your phone or computer

**Phone (iPhone or Android)**  
1. Open one of the addresses above in the browser.  
2. Tap the Share button.  
3. Choose “Add to Home Screen.”  
4. Name it (example: “Federation Core”).  
5. The icon appears. Tap it anytime.

**Computer**  
1. Open the address.  
2. Bookmark it (Ctrl+D or Cmd+D).  
3. Optional: drag the bookmark to your desktop or bookmarks bar.

You only need to do this once per site you care about.

## 5. What works even if external AI is down

The Federation keeps its own files (YAML and Markdown).  
If the paid AI services are unavailable, the system can still answer from those local files at reduced capacity.  
The design is in `BASELINE_AND_OUTBOUND.yaml` inside the mind folder.
